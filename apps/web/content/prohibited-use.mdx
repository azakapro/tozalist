# Prohibited Use Policy

TozaList exists to clean lists that businesses legitimately own. The following
uses are prohibited, are grounds for suspension, and — where the law requires —
will be reported.

## Prohibited lists

- **Purchased lists** — lists bought from a broker or another business.
- **Scraped lists** — addresses collected from websites, social networks, or
  any source the person did not submit to you.
- **Lists without a consent basis** — any list where the people on it did not
  give you their address in a business relationship with you.

## Prohibited purposes

- **Consumer, credit, employment, or insurance screening** — TozaList is not a
  consumer reporting agency and results must never inform eligibility
  decisions of that kind.
- **Harassment or stalking** — any use targeting an individual.
- **Automated decisions about people** — results must not be the basis of any
  automated decision that affects a person's access to services, employment,
  housing, credit, or insurance.

## What the service does not provide

TozaList never identifies who owns an address or a phone number, never provides
carrier data, and never enriches your data with information about people. A
request to do any of these will be refused.

## Enforcement

We review reports at salom@tozalist.uz. Violations lead to suspension and, for
consent violations, deletion of the offending data.
