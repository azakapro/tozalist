# Privacy Policy

TozaList checks email lists that our customers already own. This policy explains
what we store, why, for how long, and how to remove it.

## The short version

Your lists are yours. We process them only to produce your results, keep them
only for the retention period you choose, and never sell, share, or reuse them.

## Data map

| What we store                               | Where                        | How long                                | Why                            | How to delete                                |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Account email and Argon2id password hash    | PostgreSQL (users)           | Until account deletion                  | Sign-in                        | Delete the organization in Settings          |
| TOTP secret and hashed recovery codes       | PostgreSQL (users)           | Until account deletion                  | Two-factor auth                | Delete the organization                      |
| API key hashes and display prefixes         | PostgreSQL (api_keys)        | Until revoked + retention               | API authentication             | Revoke in the dashboard                      |
| Checked email addresses and verdicts        | PostgreSQL (email_checks)    | Your chosen retention: 7, 30 or 90 days | Your check results and caching | Automatic expiry, or delete the organization |
| Checked phone numbers (format results only) | PostgreSQL (phone_checks)    | Your chosen retention                   | Your check results             | Automatic expiry, or delete the organization |
| Uploaded and result CSV files               | S3-compatible object storage | Your chosen retention                   | Batch processing and downloads | Delete the batch, or automatic expiry        |
| Credit ledger entries                       | PostgreSQL (credit_ledger)   | Kept (financial record, no addresses)   | Accounting integrity           | Not deleted; contains no personal data       |
| Audit events (actions, not content)         | PostgreSQL (audit_events)    | 90 days                                 | Security accountability        | Automatic expiry                             |
| Pilot and contact requests                  | PostgreSQL (leads)           | Marked to expire after 180 days\*       | Replying to you                | Email us to delete sooner                    |

\* Each request is stored with a 180-day expiry date. The scheduled job
that deletes expired requests automatically is planned but not live yet;
until it is, we delete expired and requested records manually.

## What we never do

We do not sell contact data, look up who owns a number or its carrier, scrape,
or use
your lists for anything except your own checks. Verdicts are risk signals with
reasons — never statements about a person.

## Your choices

Retention is selectable per organization (7, 30 or 90 days). Deleting your
organization disables access immediately and schedules permanent removal of
your stored data. Write to salom@tozalist.uz for any privacy request.
