# Limitations

Honest tools state their limits. These are ours.

## No delivery promise

A verdict is a risk signal computed at check time. Whether a message arrives
depends on the receiving server at the moment of sending — no service can
truthfully promise delivery, and TozaList does not.

## "Unknown" and catch-all are not failures

Some servers refuse to reveal whether a mailbox exists; catch-all domains
accept mail for every address. In both cases the honest answer is `unknown`.
Keep those addresses. Deleting `unknown` results throws away real subscribers.

## Phone checks are format-only

Phone validation checks a number against its national numbering plan —
offline. It never contacts a carrier, never determines who owns a number,
and never checks whether a number is active. A `PHONE_OK` means "the format
fits", nothing more.

## Retention and deletion

Results live for the retention period your organization chooses (7, 30 or 90
days) and are then deleted, batch files included. Deleting your organization
schedules removal of everything. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy) data map.

## Not for decisions about people

Results describe addresses and numbers, not people. They must not be used for
consumer, credit, employment or insurance screening, or any automated decision
affecting a person's access to services. See the
[Prohibited Use Policy](../prohibited-use).
