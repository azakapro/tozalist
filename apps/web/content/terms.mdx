from Tashkent, Uzbekistan.

## The service

TozaList checks email addresses and phone-number formats that you submit, and
returns risk verdicts with reasons. Verdicts are signals to inform your own
decisions. We do not promise that any message will be delivered, and nothing in
the service should be read as such a promise.

## Your responsibilities

- Keep your API keys and credentials secret; rotate them if exposed.
- Do not attempt to disrupt, overload, or reverse-engineer the service.
